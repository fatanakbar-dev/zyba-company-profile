3. ZYBA DESIGN
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

ZYBA DESIGN
Designed with Purpose. Made with Comfort.

ZYBA DESIGN is a creative and production partner that develops apparel and fashion based on the unique needs, character, and identity of each client.

We don't just make products that look good. Every design is engineered with function, comfort, material quality, and user character in mind—because a great product is defined not only by its appearance, but by how it functions and feels.

From corporate uniforms, healthcare & clinic wear, restaurant & F&B uniforms, factory & industrial workwear, sports & activewear, to exclusive custom fashion, every project is tailored specifically through a process that begins with understanding needs and ends with a finished product.

We believe good design shouldn't just look right—it must feel right when worn.


[OUR PROCESS / SERVICES]
ZYBA DESIGN SERVICES
From Concept to Creation

01 - Understanding & Concept Development
We begin by deeply understanding the brand, user needs, activities, character, and product goals to define the precise design direction.

02 - Design Development
Ideas are translated into designs that carefully balance aesthetics, function, ergonomics, comfort, and brand identity.

03 - Material Selection
We never select materials at random. Fabric choice is calibrated to product type, user activity, operational environment, design character, and required comfort levels.

04 - Production & Quality Control
Designs are brought to life through controlled production processes, with rigorous attention to detail, construction, finishing, and overall consistency.

05 - Final Product
Delivering products that not only represent the brand identity but are also comfortable, functional, and aligned with user requirements.

ZYBA IMPACT
Creating More Than Just Business.

We believe business is about more than producing goods.
Business can generate opportunities, empower communities, protect the environment, and deliver forward-looking solutions.

Zyba Impact is a platform where creativity meets purpose.
We drive impact across three core areas:


[PILLARS & SPECIFIC INITIATIVES]

01 - NATURE
ZYBA RE:NEW
Rethinking What Fashion Leaves Behind.

Nature is not merely where we reside; it is the vital environment that allows us to thrive.
Through environmental initiatives and educational outreach, Zyba seeks to foster awareness and promote more responsible practices toward our ecosystem. Because protecting nature means safeguarding our future.

Fashion produces various materials that retain value—ranging from production scraps and deadstock to post-consumer garments.
Through ZYBA RE:NEW, we aim to intercept fashion waste before it becomes an environmental burden by extending material life through reuse, upcycling, and re-processing techniques.

From fashion waste to new possibilities.
[Join ZYBA RE:NEW →]


02 - SOCIAL
ZYBA EMPOWER
Empowering Women Through Skills & Opportunities.

Impact always starts with people.
We believe empowerment begins with access to learning and growth.
Through education, skill development, collaboration, and social initiatives, Zyba strives to create spaces where individuals and communities can thrive, connect, and contribute. Meaningful change happens when everyone is given the opportunity to participate.

ZYBA EMPOWER provides training and skill development programs for women in domains closely tied to fashion and lifestyle, including beauty, fashion, wellness, creative skills, and entrepreneurship.
It goes beyond teaching skills—it opens doors for women to build confidence, realize their potential, and generate new opportunities.

Learn. Create. Grow.
[Join ZYBA EMPOWER →]


03 - TECHNOLOGY
FWastezy
Turning Fashion Waste Into New Material.

Technology is not just about efficiency.
We believe innovation can serve as a powerful tool to address real-world problems, unlock fresh possibilities, and scale positive impact.

FWastezy is a proprietary technology developed by ZYBA to process fashion waste into raw materials suitable for new product creation.
By taking production cut-offs, deadstock, factory rejects, and post-consumer apparel, materials undergo multi-stage transformation using FWastezy technology.

We prevent fashion waste from ending up in landfills.
Waste is not the end. It is the beginning of a new material.
